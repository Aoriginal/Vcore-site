## Pattern Library

### Pattern: Lead with the outcome, not the process
**Evidence:** Analyzed across N conversations — messages starting with the benefit/result received X% higher engagement than process-first messages.
**Application:**
- ❌ "We've developed a new system that uses AI to analyze your data and then generates..."
- ✅ "Your team saves 3 hours/week on reporting. Here's how."
**When to use:** All outbound messages, cold or warm.
**Confidence:** Medium

---

### Pattern: Match message length to relationship depth
**Evidence:** Cold outreach > 4 sentences rarely gets responses. Established relationships tolerate longer context.
**Application:**
- Cold: 3-4 sentences max. One ask.
- Warm: 6-8 sentences OK. One ask still.
- Existing: As long as needed.
**When to use:** All outbound.
**Confidence:** High

---

### Pattern: The PS line gets read
**Evidence:** PS lines in email messages are disproportionately read — often before the body.
**Application:** Put the second most important point in a PS. Put the most important in the first line.
**When to use:** Email outreach.
**Confidence:** Medium
