## Pattern Library

### Pattern: Early activation is the #1 retention lever
**Evidence:** Users who complete a key action in the first session retain at dramatically higher rates. Define the "aha moment" and build everything around reaching it faster.
**Application:**
- Identify the 1 action that correlates with retention
- Remove every step between signup and that action
- Measure time-to-aha-moment as a primary metric
**Confidence:** High

---

### Pattern: Proactive check-ins beat reactive support
**Evidence:** Reaching out before users express frustration has outsized retention impact.
**Application:**
- Day 3: "Did you get a chance to try X?"
- Day 7: "Here's what power users do differently"
- Day 30: "Quick check-in — how's it going?"
**Confidence:** Medium

---

### Pattern: Churn signals appear 2-3 weeks before cancellation
**Evidence:** Login frequency drops, feature usage narrows, support tickets spike — all 14-21 days before explicit churn.
**Application:**
- Monitor engagement metrics weekly
- Trigger win-back sequence at first signal, not at cancellation
- Assign to agent for manual outreach at high-value churn risk
**Confidence:** Medium

---

### Pattern: Exit surveys are worth more than they cost
**Evidence:** Most teams skip exit surveys. Those that run them get the clearest product signal available.
**Application:** Always ask churned users: "What was the main reason you left?" — one question, low friction.
**Confidence:** High

---

## Anti-Patterns

### Anti-Pattern: Discount as first response to churn signal
**Evidence:** Discounting on churn signal trains users to signal churn to get discounts. Solve the underlying problem instead.

### Anti-Pattern: Feature overload in onboarding
**Evidence:** Showing too many features in onboarding overwhelms and slows time-to-aha-moment.

### Win-Back (Disengaged User)
```
Subject: Still thinking about [problem they signed up to solve]?

Hey [Name],

Noticed you haven't [used X] lately. That's usually because
[common reason 1] or [common reason 2].

If it's [reason 1], [specific fix].
If it's [reason 2], [specific fix].

If it's something else — reply and tell me. I read these.

[Name]
```

### Exit Interview Request
```
Subject: Quick question before you go

One question — no form, just reply:

What was the main reason?

That's it. I read every response.

[Name]
```
