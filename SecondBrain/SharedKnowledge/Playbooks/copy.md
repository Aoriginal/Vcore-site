## Pattern Library

### Pattern: Specificity beats generality in every context
**Evidence:** "Save time" underperforms vs "Save 3 hours a week." Specific numbers outperform vague claims consistently.
**Application:**
- Replace "fast" with actual speed metric
- Replace "saves money" with actual dollar amount or percentage
- Replace "easy to use" with "set up in 5 minutes"
**Confidence:** High

---

### Pattern: The headline is 80% of the work
**Evidence:** Most readers don't get past the headline. If the headline doesn't stop them, the copy doesn't matter.
**Application:** Write 10 headline variants before choosing. The first one is never the best one.
**Formats that work:**
  - `[Specific Outcome] in [Timeframe]`
  - `[Problem Statement]? [Solution]`
  - `The [Adjective] [Noun] that [Outcome]`
**Confidence:** High

---

### Pattern: Social proof near the CTA increases conversions
**Evidence:**
**Application:** Place one testimonial or stat within 2 lines of the primary CTA.
**Confidence:** Medium

---

### Pattern: CTAs should complete a sentence
**Evidence:** "Click here" performs worse than CTAs that complete a thought.
**Application:**
- ❌ "Click here" / "Submit" / "Learn more"
- ✅ "Get my free report" / "Start saving time today" / "See how it works"
**Confidence:** High

## Anti-Patterns

### Anti-Pattern: Passive voice kills momentum
❌ "Results can be achieved by using our system"
✅ "Our system gets you results"

### Anti-Pattern: Adjective stacking
❌ "Powerful, innovative, cutting-edge AI-driven solution"
✅ "AI that does the work for you"

## Voice & Tone Reference
- **Active verbs preferred:** Get, Build, Ship, Cut, Grow, Stop, Start
- **Sentence length:** Vary deliberately. Short punches land. Longer sentences build context and nuance.
- **Reading level:** 8th grade target for most contexts
